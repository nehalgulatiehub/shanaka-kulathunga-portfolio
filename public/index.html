Shanaka Kulathunga is an acclaimed artist and prizewinner based in Sri Lanka. With years of expertise and numerous accolades, his artistry has earned national recognition and a devoted following.

Kulathunga’s distinctive portraits delve into the beauty and lifestyles of people from diverse walks of life, ranging from rural inhabitants to contemporary fashion models. His work resonates with authenticity, portraying the soul of his subjects.

Combining a classical oil-on-canvas technique with modern themes, Kulathunga’s compositions explore the delicate harmony of design and emotion. His art speaks of a fascination with nature’s wonders and the essence of humanity.

Available for commissioned works, Shanaka Kulathunga transforms ideas into unique masterpieces. Whether it’s a personal portrait or a concept-driven project, his creations are tailored to perfection.

Shanaka Kulathunga is a figurative artist and doctor whose work delves into the profound relationship between humans and nature. His paintings bring to life the emotions, interactions, and connections that define the human experience, offering a unique blend of artistry and empathy. Through his evocative imagery, Shanaka captures moments of struggle, triumph, and nostalgia, inviting viewers to reflect on the complexities of life. With a medical background enriching his artistic perspective, his creations resonate deeply, bridging art and life seamlessly.
